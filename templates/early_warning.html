How it is measured

  • Flagged is the first moment the rules, seeing only what had happened by then, said at risk or delayed.
  • The carrier admits a problem at the earliest of a scan that says so (exception, held, customs hold, delivery attempted), the carrier's own ETA moving past the promise, or the promise time passing without a delivery. Carrier notes are free text and are never used.
  • If the carrier admitted it before the shipment had both a scan and an ETA to look at, the admission is counted from that first look.
  • Shipments the rules cannot assess, flagged ones that recovered, and false alarms are listed separately and are not averaged in.
  • This measures this dataset only. It says nothing about how real carriers behave.
